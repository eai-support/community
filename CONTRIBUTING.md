Thank you for improving EnterpriseAI's public community resources.

## Before contributing

- Use Help & Q&A for questions and Ideas & Product Feedback for proposals.
- Use a structured Issue for a reproducible defect.
- Use private security reporting for vulnerabilities and authenticated support
  for confidential matters.
- Confirm you have permission to publish every example, link, and asset.

## Public-data boundary

Never commit or post credentials, personal or customer data, tenant
identifiers, confidential diagnostics, private URLs, unsanitized logs,
vulnerability details, contract attributes, or private work references. Use
synthetic examples. If sensitive material appears, stop and follow the
sensitive-disclosure runbook.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused and explain the public customer outcome. Identify affected
routes, policies, forms, workflows, permissions, and rollback. Run policy,
link, form-schema, public-boundary, and workflow-permission checks.

Changes to policies, routes, templates, security settings, or workflows require
two approvals, a Code Owner review after protected owner inputs are supplied,
resolved review conversations, and the required checks. External-contributor
workflows receive read-only permissions, no secrets, and maintainer approval.

## Review and decisions

Maintainers assess correctness, accessibility, safety, maintainability, and
consistency with the public/private boundary. Acceptance does not create a
product roadmap, release date, support entitlement, or delivery commitment.

Contributions follow the Code of Conduct and the governance policy. By
submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to provide it
under the repository's Legal-approved license; customer promotion remains
blocked until that license is supplied.
