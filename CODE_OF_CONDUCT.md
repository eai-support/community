## Our commitment

EnterpriseAI is committed to a respectful, inclusive, accessible, and
harassment-free public community. This policy applies to Issues, Discussions,
pull requests, comments, events, and other spaces represented as part of the
`eai-tools` community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and outcomes, not people.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and the public-data boundary.
- Use accessible language and provide context that others can reuse.
- Follow moderator direction and use the correct public or private route.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misgendering, sustained disruption, spam, impersonation, publication of private
information, and attempts to obtain credentials or confidential customer data
are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not disclose conduct reports that contain personal or sensitive information
in a public thread. Use the approved private support route in `SUPPORT.md`.
Vulnerability details use the separate restricted route in `SECURITY.md`.

Moderators apply the published moderation runbook and choose the least
restrictive proportionate response: guidance, edit request, minimization, lock,
report, temporary interaction limit, or reviewed account restriction. A private
record contains only the minimum sanitized rationale and never republishes the
reported content.

## Appeals

A participant may request private review through the approved support route.
An independent moderator who did not make the original decision reviews the
appeal and may uphold, modify, or reverse the action. Temporary limits include
an expiry and restoration check.

This policy does not replace GitHub's Terms of Service or Acceptable Use
Policies.
