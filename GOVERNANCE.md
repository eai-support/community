## Scope

This policy governs the public `eai-tools` organization profile, community
support hub, community-health defaults, intake forms, public automation,
taxonomy, moderation guidance, and customer-safe operating evidence.

## Roles

Community, Triage, Product, Security, Support, Documentation, and Moderation
are accountable public roles. Protected launch input resolves each to a
different primary and backup principal, minimum permission, opaque notification
destination, and timed escalation. Public files never name personal on-call
details.

## Decisions

Routine corrections use pull requests and documented review. Material changes
to routes, public/private boundaries, supported scope, commitments, security,
licensing, automation permissions, retention, or moderation require the
accountable role and any applicable Security, Support, Documentation, Brand, or
Legal approval.

Two approvals, Code Owner review, resolved conversations, and required checks
protect the default branch. Organization Issue types and fields are read-only
for the initial launch. Browser-admin changes require before/after evidence and
independent second-person review.

## Transparency and commitments

Public decisions explain customer-safe outcomes and alternatives without
revealing private work, customer identity, security detail, internal schedules,
or contract data. Ideas, reactions, and accepted contributions do not promise a
roadmap, release, support level, or delivery date.

## Review, succession, and rollback

Policies are reviewed quarterly and after a material change. A role handoff is
complete only after the replacement passes access and notification tests.
Automation has a documented manual fallback and non-destructive rollback;
public Issues and Discussions are preserved.

Policy disputes and conduct appeals receive independent review through the
appropriate private route. Security matters remain isolated from general
governance and community automation.
