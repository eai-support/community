## Public community support

GitHub Issues and Discussions are public, non-contractual community channels.
Use them for customer-safe questions, reusable answers, feedback, and
reproducible defects. They do not replace authenticated customer support and do
not create response, resolution, roadmap, release-date, or service
commitments.

Remove credentials, personal or customer data, tenant identifiers, confidential
account details, private URLs, unsanitized logs, and vulnerability details
before posting. If information is already public, follow the
sensitive-disclosure runbook rather than attempting to recover confidentiality
by editing or deleting the thread.

## Authenticated customer support

<!-- route:private-support -->
Use the approved authenticated-private route for account-specific,
contractual, tenant, incident, entitlement, or confidential diagnostic matters.
The primary and fallback destinations are approval-pending and are injected by
the launch renderer only after Support ownership, access, and route checks pass.

Do not publish confidential support material in GitHub while a route is
unavailable. Use only the approved authenticated fallback.

## Service status

<!-- route:service-status -->
Use the approved authoritative public status route for current service
availability. Its destination and fallback are approval-pending. A GitHub Issue
or Discussion is not an incident-status system.

## Security

Suspected vulnerabilities use the restricted-private route in `SECURITY.md`,
not public community support or ordinary authenticated case discussion.
